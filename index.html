Privacy Policy for Guess The Synonym

Guess The Synonym respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Guess The Synonym does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the app may use third-party services that collect certain information automatically.

2. Local Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores certain gameplay information locally on your device to support app functionality, including:

  • Coins
  • Hints
  • Time extensions
  • Level unlock progress

This information is stored only to provide the app experience and is not sold by us.

3. Advertising

Guess The Synonym uses Google AdMob to display ads, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads.

Google AdMob may collect and use certain data, which may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • App interactions
  • Advertising data

This data may be used to provide, improve, and personalize ads.

To learn more about how Google uses data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Policies

4. In-App Purchases

Guess The Synonym offers in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not directly collect or store your payment information. Payment information is handled by Apple according to Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

To learn more, please visit: Apple Privacy Policy

5. Children’s Privacy

Guess The Synonym does not knowingly collect personal information from children directly. However, third-party advertising providers may collect certain device-related data as described above.

Parents or guardians who have questions may contact us using the contact information below.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party service providers only as necessary to operate the app, including:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Apple for in-app purchases and app distribution

7. Your Choices

You may stop using the app at any time. You may also reset local app data by deleting the app from your device.

You can manage ad-related permissions and privacy choices through your device settings where available.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at the updated policy URL with a revised effective date.
